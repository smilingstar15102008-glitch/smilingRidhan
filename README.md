## 📖 How to Play

### Characters
Choose from three unique heroes with different playstyles:

- **🗡️ Warrior** - Balanced fighter with high HP and moderate damage
- **🔮 Mage** - Powerful spellcaster with high damage but low HP
- **🗹 Rogue** - Fast and agile with quick attacks and medium HP

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mobile |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Move Left | ← or A | Left side |
| Move Right | → or D | Right side |
| Jump | ↑ or W or Space | Top area |
| Attack | Z | Bottom-left |
| Special Attack | S | Bottom-right |
| Dodge | X | Bottom center |

### Gameplay

1. **Fight 7 Waves** - Defeat increasingly difficult enemies
2. **Defeat Bosses** - Every 3rd wave spawns a powerful boss enemy
3. **Collect Potions** - Health potions drop from defeated enemies
4. **Build Combos** - Consecutive hits increase your combo multiplier for bonus points
5. **Upgrade** - After each wave, choose an upgrade:
   - ❤️ **+30 Max HP** - Heal fully
   - ⚔️ **+8 Damage** - Increase damage output
   - ⚡ **Faster Special** - Reduce special ability cooldown
